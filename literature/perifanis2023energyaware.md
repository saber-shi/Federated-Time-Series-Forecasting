- Authors: Vasileios Perifanis, Nikolaos Pavlidis, Selim F. Yilmaz, Francesc Wilhelmi, Elia Guerra, Marco Miozzo, Pavlos S. Efraimidis, Paolo Dini, Remous-Aris Koutsiamanis

## Relevance

This paper introduces energy-awareness into the forecasting workflow, which makes it the natural bridge between predictive performance and deployment cost.
